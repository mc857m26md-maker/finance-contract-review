# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We welcome finance, legal-operations, engineering, and open-source contributors of every background. Participate with respect, focus criticism on the work, explain disagreements with evidence, and protect confidential information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of confidential or personal information, and sustained disruption of project discussions.

## Project-specific privacy rule

Do not post real contracts, bank details, personal data, credentials, privileged material, customer information, or confidential company playbooks. Maintainers may remove such material immediately to protect affected people and organizations.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Security or privacy-sensitive reports should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not public issues.
