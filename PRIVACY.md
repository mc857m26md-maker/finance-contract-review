# Privacy

Finance Contract Review is a skills-only package. It does not operate a server, create an account, collect telemetry, or transmit contract text by itself.

The host product, model provider, connected tools, operating system, and storage location may process data under their own terms and settings. Users are responsible for confirming that their chosen environment is approved for the contract and company data involved.

Do not put real contracts, personal information, payment credentials, bank details, privileged material, or confidential company playbooks in public issues, pull requests, examples, or discussions.

Use synthetic data for bug reports. Security concerns should be reported as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
