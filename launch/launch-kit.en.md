## Long-form launch post

### Title

Open source: a finance-first contract review skill that recalculates the deal instead of only summarizing it

### Body

I built **Finance Contract Review**, an open-source agent skill and plugin for controllers, procurement finance, FP&A, accounts payable, shared services, and finance business partners.

It focuses on finance-control failures that generic contract summaries can miss:

- amount, tax, line-item, and milestone reconciliation;
- payment linkage to invoice, delivery, and acceptance evidence;
- renewals, price increases, rebates, refunds, and minimum commitments;
- independent verification of bank-detail changes;
- escalation to legal, tax, treasury, accounting policy, or authorized approvers;
- post-signature obligations with owners and dates.

The repository includes a fully fictional agreement and a worked review. The example exposes a total that does not reconcile, milestones totaling 110%, deemed acceptance, email-only bank changes, and an automatic 15% renewal increase.

MIT licensed. No real contract data is required, and the project does not replace qualified professional review.
